OFFICIAL STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE

ISKCON Ponda Kitchen Policy & Payroll Base

Official guidelines approved by the Temple Sub-Committee for sanctified cooking, deity bhoga standards, staff roles, machine maintenance, vendor purchasing, and cook payroll/incentive distribution.

Download Policy PDF (8 Pages)
1

Spiritual Sanctity & Daily Schedule

Bhoga offering & hygiene discipline
  • Untouched Bhoga: Everything cooked must be first offered to the Lord. Mixing unoffered food with prasadam is strictly prohibited.
  • Personal Cleanliness: All cooking must be done strictly after a proper bath. Another bath is mandatory after afternoon rest.
  • Required Attire: Dhoti and Kurta only. Apron and hair caps are mandatory while cooking and must be washed nightly.
  • Offering Timing: Bhoga must be prepared and ready at least 15 minutes prior to scheduled offering time.
  • Brahmachari Utensils: Prasadam for Brahmacharis must always be kept in separate sanctified utensils.

4

Compensation, Benefits & Yearly Package

Base salary, festival incentives, PF and allowances
  • Monthly Base Salary: Fixed at approx ₹18,000/month.
  • Major Festival Allowance: ₹1,000 per program for cooking on major festival days (Janmashtami, Radhashtami, Gaura Purnima).
  • Yearly Janmāṣṭamī Bonus: ₹5,000 annual bonus.
  • Annual Increment: 6% yearly increment (effective from April 2026).
  • Medical Assistance & ESIC: ₹8,000 per year emergency assistance and ESIC coverage.
  • Annual Clothing: 3 dhotis, 3 kurtas, 3 aprons, 3 caps, and 3 masks provided annually.
  • Annual Travel: Sleeper class train tickets home & return journey sponsored by temple for kitchen employees.
  • Total Annual Package: Estimated at ₹3,75,000 / year per cook.
5

Leave, Weekly-Off & Order Rules

Operational policies for orders and staff leave
  • Weekly Off (Wednesday): Only routine cleaning and basic cooking are performed. Strictly no orders accepted on Wednesdays.
  • Paid Leave: 1 month paid leave per year. Unused leave can be encashed as an extra month's salary at the end of the financial year.
  • Custom Menu Orders: Must be placed at least 48 hours in advance.
  • Advance Payment: 100% advance payment required directly into ISKCON Ponda account via UPI or Net Banking (cheques not accepted).
  • No Home Delivery: Devotees must arrange pickup directly from ISKCON Ponda Kitchen.
  • Notice Period: Mandatory notice period of 2 months when leaving service.
6

Devotee Interaction Guidelines & Sub-Committee Sign-Off

Approved governance and authority structure

The temple kitchen is meant for serving the Lord and all devotees equally. Private exchanges of money, loans, or seeking special personal prasadam through cooks is strictly prohibited. All payments, orders, and feedback must be routed exclusively through the Kitchen In-Charge (9 AM–9 PM).
